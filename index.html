How to Pay

Follow these steps on your phone to complete payment.

1
Open M-Pesa

Go to the M-Pesa menu on your phone (SIM toolkit or M-Pesa app).

2
Select Lipa na M-Pesa

Choose Lipa na M-Pesa from the M-Pesa menu options.

3
Select Pochi la Biashara

Choose Pochi la Biashara (not Paybill or Buy Goods).

4
Enter Business Number

Type 0795999639 as the business phone number.

5
Enter the Amount

Type the exact total shown on the left: KES 0

6
Confirm with PIN

Enter your M-Pesa PIN to complete. You will receive a confirmation SMS.
